The Challenge
How can we use an intuitive, AI-powered Android app to create clear, step-by-step visual work instructions (e.g., based on the TEACCH approach) that can be exported as PDFs and imported into Moodle – reducing the workload for training staff while helping learners complete tasks and develop their skills as independently as possible?

Caritas-Don Bosco GmbH in Würzburg is a non-profit educational organization jointly operated by the German Province of the Salesians of Don Bosco and the Caritas Association of the Diocese of Würzburg. It serves as a regional and supra-regional center for education and vocational training.

Vocational Training Center (BBW) Würzburg
The Vocational Training Center (Berufsbildungswerk, BBW) supports young people with additional learning and support needs as they prepare for working life. Its programs are designed for young people with mental health conditions, autism spectrum conditions, or specific learning support needs. A range of residential options provides personalized educational guidance and everyday support. The overall goal is to help young people develop meaningful career opportunities and personal prospects.

To carry out practical training tasks and work processes independently and accurately, many participants need highly structured, easy-to-follow instructions with clear text and visual guidance. Visual teaching methods such as TEACCH are particularly helpful for learners on the autism spectrum. Currently, creating these instructions takes a significant amount of time and effort. Training staff must manually take photos, transfer and format them, and add simple, easy-to-understand explanations.

We are looking for a mobile AI-powered instruction generator in the form of an Android app that allows staff to create work instructions directly at the training location.
The app should integrate photos, automatically generate clear and accessible step-by-step instructions, optionally connect tasks to relevant vocational curricula, and export the results both as print-ready PDFs in the organization's corporate design and as import packages for the Moodle learning platform.

The Goal
The goal is to develop a practical prototype of an intuitive, preferably Android-based application that helps vocational trainers and educational staff create high-quality, accessible work instructions.
The following features are particularly desirable:

1. AI-Powered Instruction Creation and Educational Structuring
Generate clear, step-by-step work instructions from photos, keywords, or existing documents.

Automatically create simple, easy-to-understand instructions based on the TEACCH approach.
Adapt language, length, and visual presentation to different learning abilities and individual support needs.
Help select and organize suitable photos, symbols, and pictograms.
2. AI-Powered Alignment with Vocational Training Curricula

Analyze which skills, competencies, and learning objectives from a vocational training curriculum are covered by a specific practical task.
Connect hands-on work assignments with relevant theoretical content taught in vocational schools.
Suggest additional explanations, technical terms, or simple comprehension questions.
Provide transparent references to the curriculum content and sources used.
3. AI-Powered Quality Assurance

Automatically review existing or newly created instructions based on defined professional and educational quality criteria.
Check instructions for clarity, completeness, logical step-by-step sequences, and an appropriate level of complexity.
Provide specific suggestions for improvement to help learners complete tasks as independently as possible.
Allow training staff to review, edit, and approve all instructions before they are finalized.
4. Easy Content Creation and Export

Capture and integrate photos directly at the workplace or training location.
Automatically generate print-ready PDF documents that follow the organization's corporate design.
Ideally, export content in a Moodle-compatible format (e.g., H5P, SCORM, or Moodle Lesson).
For the hackathon, the main priority is to develop and demonstrate the core AI features in a working prototype. A fully developed Android app, offline synchronization, and complete Moodle integration can be implemented in a later development phase if needed.

Specific Requirements

Interfaces and Export: Seamless export to PDF and Moodle using standard import formats.
Data Privacy and GDPR Compliance: Strict adherence to data protection requirements, particularly when taking photos. The privacy of participants and staff must be protected, for example by focusing images on workpieces or automatically blurring faces.
Offline Functionality: Reliable content capture even in workshops with unstable Wi-Fi connections, with the option to synchronize data once connectivity is restored.
Ease of Use: An intuitive, user-friendly interface, ideally with a guided step-by-step workflow (e.g., a wizard-style interface). 

Additional Information
Special attention is given to the target group of young people with autism spectrum disorder or learning disabilities: The program structure must prevent sensory overload and instill confidence in their ability to act.
